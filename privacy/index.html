Privacy Policy

Little Latches for iPhone · Effective October 17, 2026

Little Latches is made by Brandon Sooknanan ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information. In short: we do not collect any personal data.

What stays on your device

The game saves your puzzle progress, stars, coins, boosters, settings and current puzzle on your iPhone so you can continue where you left off. This information stays on your device. We do not run player accounts or a sync service, and we never receive your gameplay records.

No ads, tracking or analytics

Little Latches contains no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising identifier.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive your name, card number or other payment details. The game uses Apple's verified purchase information only to provide what you bought, and keeps a small record on your device so an interrupted purchase is delivered safely. Restore Purchases brings back permanent purchases through Apple.

Backups and deleting your data

Your iPhone's backups may include the game's saved progress, according to your Apple backup settings. Deleting the app removes its saved progress from that device. Your App Store purchase history is kept by Apple.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If you email us

If you contact support by email, we use your message and email address only to reply and help you. We do not share it or use it for marketing.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@statementready.net
